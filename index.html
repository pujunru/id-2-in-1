Auto levels stretches each side so its darkest part prints black and its lightest prints white — the quickest fix for dark phone photos. Brightness lifts the midtones without washing out the whites; Contrast separates text from background. The previews and the page update as you drag.

Auto-crop finds the card by scanning in from the scanner background (usually white/black) and trims to the first strong edge. If it grabs too much or too little, nudge Edge sens. and hit Re-crop — or tick Manual crop and drag a box on the preview to set it yourself.

One photo · 2 × 2 in

The sheet is 1800 × 1200 px at 300 dpi and every photo occupies exactly 600 × 600 px, so each prints at exactly 2 × 2 in when the sheet prints at 4 × 6 in. All three downloads carry that size: the JPEG and PNG are tagged 300 dpi and the PDF page is exactly 6 × 4 in. At a photo lab, order a standard 4×6 print; the 3:2 sheet matches the paper exactly, so nothing is cropped. On a home printer, choose 4×6 paper, borderless, and scale 100% / Actual size — never “Fit to page”.
